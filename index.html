Sync across devices

  1. Create a GitHub token. Choose Only select repositories and pick tasks-data. Under Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  2. Paste the token here and press Connect. Do this once on each device.

How it works

  • Type a task and press Enter. The box stays ready for the next one. Press N to jump to it.
  • Say or type when: Thursday, next Thursday, tomorrow, next week, 5 Nov, in 3 days, someday. The task goes to that day.
  • A list becomes separate tasks: call Ian, send Pete the deck and book the car service. Check the preview, or press Shift+Enter to add it exactly as typed.
  • The calendar button in the box sets the day for everything you add until you change it.
  • Swipe a task with a finger, or two fingers on a trackpad. Left ticks it off. All the way right pushes it to the next working day. Halfway right lets you pick a day.
  • Drag a task to reorder it or move it to another day. Day buttons appear at the bottom while you drag. On a phone, press and hold first.
  • Anything not done moves to today by itself. After three days it's marked overdue.
  • Click a task to edit it. Clear the text to delete it.
